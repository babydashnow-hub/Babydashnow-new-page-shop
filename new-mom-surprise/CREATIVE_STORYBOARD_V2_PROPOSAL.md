## 2. Opening assessment

**Is the handwriting opening strong enough? Yes as an image, but not on its own as a readable line within 2 s.**
- The first 1.6 s show a hand writing on cream paper at a warm wooden desk: human, intimate, curiosity-raising ("who is she writing to?").
- The full heading is readable for only 0.2 s, too short to read at feed speed.

**Options (decision needed):**

| Option | What it does | Trade-off |
|---|---|---|
| **O1 (recommended):** camera original of A | Obtain the founder's wife's original iPhone recording of the handwriting; it likely contains a longer, uncut moment with the heading visible | Needs the file; it also fixes the 1440p resolution |
| O2: hold the last frame | A 10.47 → 12.30, then hold frame 368 for about 1.0 s so "For a special new mom." can be read | A held frame is a mild editorial device, not a visual effect, but it's **not natural motion**. Needs founder approval. |
| O3: let the line arrive with text | Play A 10.47 → 12.30 as recorded; a quiet on-screen line carries the meaning | Adds words; the handwriting is felt rather than read |
| **Alternative opening (existing footage):** doorstep first | P 0.0 → 1.8: the gift waiting at a door ("who left this?"), then back to the handwriting | Stronger feed hook as a question, but static. It spends the payoff early and makes the film a bookend. |

**Recommendation:** O1 if the original exists; otherwise O2 (with approval). Keep the doorstep-first option in reserve.

**Pacing:**
- Shot 1 is unhurried; shots 2–8 move steadily (1.6–2.0 s each, as gestures rather than products).

**Products deliberately not used:** pacifiers (F) and duplicate holds. They add catalogue feel without new emotion. Can be reinstated if needed for length.
