## 4. Technical and creative risks

1. **Letter privacy:** B shows the whole letter legibly. Use A (unreadable) or a partial crop of B only.
2. **Privacy:** licence plates (N, O) and the identifiable residence (O). Recommend dropping O and either dropping N or blurring its plate.
3. **No recipient footage:** no door opening, handover or reaction exists. The ending must stop at the doorstep, and copy must not imply a recorded reaction or a completed hand-off.
4. **Continuity:** the Huggies, Pampers, plush and Johnson's items appear in the car (K) and at the door (P) but never in the packing. That's fine if they were delivered alongside the gift box, but captions shouldn't say "everything in this box".
5. **Third-party marks:** SwaddleMe, MAM, Aquaphor, Aveeno, Huggies, Pampers, Johnson's and Toyota RAV4.
6. **HDR source:** needs a careful HLG → SDR conversion (the method validated on the Brand Reel). The fill in C and the warm desk can shift toward red or orange.
7. **Motion blur** at every product entry: cut on the settled frames listed above.
8. **Driving shots:** the mount sits in the foreground; M and N need a tight crop or a short duration.
9. **A** is a 1440×2560 re-export: acceptable for 1080 output. Is a camera original available?
10. **Frame rate:** J and L are 29.97 fps (others 30). That's a minor conform, with no visible issue at these durations.

## 5. Missing footage

- **A clean exterior hero of the closed BabyDashNow box** (only the angled seal shot J exists).
- **The letter being folded and placed in the box.** Nothing links the letter (A/B) to the box; the card in I is a different item.
- **Any arrival or hand-off moment** (by design or not, see risk 3). Also no shot of placing items at the door, only the finished arrangement.
- **A steady "on the way" shot** without the phone mount.
- **The extra items** (diapers, wipes, plush, Johnson's) being packed or loaded.

## 6. Questions for the creative director

1. Who is "someone"? Is the gift from BabyDashNow or on behalf of a person, and can the story say so?
2. Is the letter BabyDashNow's own message? What, if any, part may be readable on screen?
3. Do you have the mother's consent to show her doorstep and building? (I recommend excluding O.)
4. Should the ending stay at the doorstep (P 0–3 s), or do you have consented footage of the hand-off?
5. Are the visible third-party brands acceptable?
6. Should we cut the dashboard (L) and street (N) shots?
7. Is the camera original of A available, and are there unshot inserts (closed-box hero, letter going into the box) that you can still film?

## G. Technical delivery plan (no conversion performed)

- **Source:** HEVC 10-bit, HLG / BT.2020, 2160×3840 9:16 (A is 1440×2560 9:16).
- **HDR vs SDR:**
  - Instagram and TikTok accept iPhone HDR uploads, but playback is device-dependent. SDR phones and some in-app paths get the platform's own uncontrolled tone mapping. Facebook support is inconsistent, and text or brand graphics in an HDR stream can render overly bright.
  - **Recommendation:**
    1. A **10-bit HLG HEVC master** made only by cutting the originals, with no conversion, as the archival and HDR-capable version.
    2. **One SDR BT.709 delivery file** for all three platforms, made with a **standards-based, parameter-fixed BT.2100 HLG → BT.709 conversion** (reference white 203 nits per ITU-R BT.2408), applied identically to every shot, with no creative adjustment.
  - This is the most faithful *predictable* result. Validate it side by side against the originals on an iPhone before publishing. Optionally test the HLG master on Instagram for iOS.
- **Frame and aspect:** keep native 9:16 with **no crop or reframe**. One high-quality downscale to 1080×1920.
- **Frame rate:** 30 fps. J and A-adjacent 29.97 material conforms to 30 with no visible change. No interpolation.
- **Audio:** use each clip's **stereo AAC** track (not the 4-channel spatial track). Preserve ambient sound, with only short fades at cuts to avoid clicks. Target about −14 to −16 LUFS integrated, −1 dBTP, using gain only. Music only if separately approved.
- **Export:** H.264 High (or HEVC Main10 for the HLG master), 1080×1920, 30 fps, high bitrate (about 12–16 Mbps), BT.709 tagged. Originals stay read-only and are never overwritten.
