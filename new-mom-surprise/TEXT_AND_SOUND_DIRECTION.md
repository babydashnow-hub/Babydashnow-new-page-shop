# D. Text and sound direction: "For a Special New Mom"

**Status:** proposal only. No text, audio or music has been created.

## 1. On-screen text (minimal, accurate, mobile-readable)

**Principles:**
- **The handwriting is the title.** Don't duplicate it.
- Never state or imply that the **gift purchaser** wrote the letter. The letter is from the **founder's wife**, and it **was included** (confirmed).
- No claims beyond the facts: someone arranged the gift; BabyDashNow prepared and delivered it; a handwritten note from the founder's family was included.
- Type: Fraunces (headline) / Inter (small line) in BabyDashNow ink or ivory, 44–56 px on 1080 wide (88–112 px on a 4K master). Lower-middle safe area (y ≈ 1100–1350 on 1080×1920). Plain fade or straight-cut appearance; no animation.

**Proposed sequence (choose one option per slot):**

| When | Option A (recommended) | Option B |
|---|---|---|
| Shot 1 (only if opening O3 is chosen) | "Someone wanted her to know she wasn't alone." | "Someone thought of her today." |
| Shot 10, at about 1.0 s into the hold | **"Someone thought of you today."** | "For a new mom, sometimes the smallest gesture means everything." *(longer: needs about 3 s)* |
| Shot 10, at about 3.0 s (small) | **"Prepared and delivered on their behalf by BabyDashNow™, with a handwritten note from our family to hers."** | "Prepared with care by BabyDashNow™. Handwritten note included." |
| Shot 10, last about 1.5 s (signature) | **BabyDashNow™** (wordmark) · *We show up when it matters* | Wordmark only (the line already appears on the seal in shot 8) |

**Notes:**
- "From our family to hers" is accurate (the founder's wife) and warm, without naming her. Please confirm she agrees to that description.
- **No price, discount, product names or "order now" language.** This is a brand story, not a promotion.
- Total words on screen: about 20–25, mostly in the final hold.

**Closing signature:**
- Not the six-second animated outro. Its motion would break the quiet ending.
- A **static wordmark** on the final doorstep frame (or a 1.0 s cut to ivory with the wordmark), held about 1.5 s.

## 2. Sound

### Source audio: must be cleared first

- **No source audio may be used publicly until reviewed.**
- **Check:** intelligible speech (especially **clip A** and anything recorded in the car or at the building), **radio or music** (car clips K, L, M), names, addresses and phone notifications.
- **Who:** ideally the founder listens to each selected range on headphones. Alternatively, authorize a technical scan (speech/music detection) that reports time ranges only.
- **Usable textures if clean:**
  - Shot 1: pen nib on paper.
  - Shot 3: crinkle-paper rustle.
  - Shot 6: tissue settling.
  - Shot 8: the soft press of the seal.

  Use at natural level with short fades at cuts. Never add sound that wasn't recorded.

### Original music (to be composed only after approval)

- **Character:** intimate, unhurried, warm, hopeful. Not sentimental, not "stock emotional".
- **Instrumentation:** solo felt piano, or felt piano with a single nylon-string guitar. Optional very low, sustained cello note in the final hold. **No vocals, drums, risers or swelling strings.**
- **Tempo and key:** about 66–72 bpm, in a major key with gentle suspensions. G major pairs with BabyDashNow's existing chime if it's ever used, though the chime is **not** proposed here.

**Structure (about 21 s):**

| Time | Music |
|---|---|
| 0–1.8 s (handwriting) | **Silence or near-silence**: only the pen. Lets the human moment breathe. |
| 1.8 s (empty box) | First notes enter, sparse: a single line |
| 2–12 s (preparation) | A simple, slowly unfolding phrase; gestures land roughly on phrase points (not hard sync) |
| 12–16 s (card → seal) | Warmth increases by one added voice, not by volume |
| 16–17 s (car) | A short breath, a held chord |
| 17–21 s (doorstep) | The phrase **resolves**, then decays naturally to silence under the closing text. The final 1 s is quiet. |

- **Mix:** music under natural sound, never covering the pen or paper. About −16 LUFS integrated, −1 dBTP, no limiter pumping. Audition on phone speakers.
- **Rights:** fully original, or composed under written licence covering Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and YouTube, including paid use.
