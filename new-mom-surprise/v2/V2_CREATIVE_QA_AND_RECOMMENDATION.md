## Assessment

| Criterion | Finding |
|---|---|
| **Opening (first 2 s)** | Strong. A real hand writing, plus "For a special new mom.", establishes who this is for before any product appears. The handwritten heading then confirms it on paper (about 3.7 s). No freeze frame or fabricated writing was needed. |
| **Emotional progression** | Intention → preparation → personal care → delivery → fulfilment, in that order. The products appear as gestures (pressing, folding, sealing), not as a catalogue. The pacifiers were deliberately left out. |
| **Pacing** | Unhurried at both ends (handwriting about 3.8 s; doorstep 5 s); the middle moves steadily at 1.6–2 s per gesture. Total 21.47 s, within the 20–23 s target. |
| **Ending** | Honest and complete: the gift at her door, the closing line, then the signature. No handoff or reaction implied. No animated outro. |
| **Text** | Three short moments only, mobile-readable, inside the organic safe zones. |
| **Letter attribution** | The film doesn't say who wrote the letter, so it doesn't misattribute it, and only the heading is visible (the letter body never appears in clip A). The letter is shown being written but **not** placed in the box, so no inclusion moment is fabricated. |
