# PASS THE CARE™ "Full Circle": Organic v6, audio review version

**Status: AUDIO REVIEW VERSION. Not final and not for publication.** It must be reviewed on a physical phone first.

- **Picture:** Organic v6, unchanged. I compared every frame's checksum against the v6 candidate: **0 of 500 frames differ**.
- **What changed:** only the story soundtrack (0.00–10.67 s).
- **Outro audio:** the official outro's own audio, including the approved chime, is used as is.

**Design rules followed:**
- One instrument family (felt piano), with the notes tuned to G major so that the official chime (G5/D6) resolves the whole phrase.
- No whooshes, impacts, jingles or cartoon effects.
- Every sound is tied to a movement of the dot, and the offer text never competes with a loud sound.

**Inherited from the official outro:** about 1.9 s of silence before the chime (10.67–12.57 s) is the outro master's own audio and is left untouched.
